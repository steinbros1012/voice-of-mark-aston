Community Guidelines

Last updated October 29, 2026

Beep Me works because students trust each other. These rules keep it that way.

Be who you say you are

Use your real name and a photo of yourself. One account per person. Never share your login code.

Show up

Honor accepted bookings. If plans change, cancel in the app as early as you can so the seat can go to someone else.

Drive safely

No phone in hand, no alcohol or drugs, seat belts for everyone, no more passengers than belts.

Keep it respectful

No harassment, discrimination, threats, or unwanted advances, in the car or in chat. No sharing of another student’s personal information.

Keep it on the platform

Arrange and pay for rides through the app. Off-app payments are not protected and void the cancellation and refund policy.

Report problems

Use Report on a ride or profile, or email support@beepme.app. In an emergency call 911.
